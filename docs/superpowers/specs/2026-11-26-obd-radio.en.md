# OBD connection and Bluetooth radio

Goal: real ELM327 readings over BLE, Android SPP and Wi-Fi; the radio serves as the phone audio output. An ordinary A2DP/HFP radio is not a diagnostic adapter.

Automatically read ECU data and faults after initialization. Multiple ECUs require selection of an actually discovered address. Live Data polls supported measurements only, without scanning faults again. Full reports retain their own timestamp and scope.

Bound the BLE startup banner to 8192 ASCII characters before the first command. Disconnection invalidates the session. Clearing requires a new full report after a Live Data measurement.

Radio pairing and output selection remain with the OS. Provide Android Bluetooth settings, iOS instructions and an explicit speech playback test. A playback request must not be presented as a confirmed radio connection.

Assumption: the phone sends audio to the radio. The question about Android head units remains open. Without physical hardware, tests cannot confirm every adapter or simultaneous Bluetooth profile operation.
